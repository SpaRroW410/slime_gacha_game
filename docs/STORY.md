# Story: *The Great Gloop*

## Premise

Long ago the world of **Gloop Hollow** was ruled by **the Mother Gloop**, a continent-sized slime whose
body *was* the land — hills were her shoulders, rivers her slow pulse. When the **Hollow Kings** tried to
bottle her power into crowns, she shattered into a million shimmering droplets and slept.

Every droplet remembered a *job*. Some remembered swinging axes (**Barbarians**), some spells (**Mages**),
some mending (**Medics**), some shadows (**Rogues**), some distance (**Rangers**), and some… the quiet
edge between life and gloop (**Necromancers**).

## You

You are the **Keeper of the Last Gacha Well** — an old wishing-well-shaped machine that "pulls"
droplets back out of the dreaming Mother. You don't command heroes; you *invite* them. Every pull is a
slime waking up, confused, hungry, and a little opinionated.

## The Stakes

The **Dry Tide** is spreading: a creeping drought that turns slimes to dust-crust. Only a gathered,
fed, rested, and *emotionally functional* colony can push it back to the **Heart of the Hollow**,
where the Mother sleeps.

## The Memorial Pond

Slimes are not immortal. A slime that starves, wanders into the Crust Road too wounded, or falls in battle
dissolves back into the Mother Gloop; the Keeper marks them in the **Memorial Pond**, and the colony grieves
(friends most of all). **White gel** is the plentiful, everyday ooze of the Mother; **purple gel** is a rare, warm shard of her
heart — dungeons almost never give it up, and the Parched King hoards it.

## The Academy

Behind the Well stands the **Academy of Plip**: a Blade Yard, an Archery Range, and the glowing Mana Pool. Beneath it
lies the **Library of Moist Pages**, where the Mother's memories of the four elements — Fire, Water, Earth and Wind —
are filed. Only droplets with at least three stars of the Mother's memory can *read* them, but any class may: a
barbarian who mixes Fire and Wind into **Lightning** is just a very loud mage. The Library keeps every new spell
a slime invents, so the colony's magic grows with its mistakes.

## The Two Dungeons

- **The Gloop Pit** — an old training hollow where the Mother's echoes spar with fresh droplets. Slimes who
  lose are merely "squished flat" and bounce back. The place to grow strong, slowly and boringly.
- **The Hollow Descent** — the Hollow Kings' shaft, a staircase into the Dry Tide. Every five floors a guardian
  waits (the **Hollow Warden**, later the **Parched King** himself). It does not forgive.

## Themes (and how they map to mechanics)

| Theme | Mechanic |
|-------|----------|
| Hunger, rest, belonging | Maslow-style needs engine; unmet needs lower mood and combat output |
| Slimes are *people* | Personalities, grudges, and conflicts you mediate |
| Merging is bittersweet | A merged slime shares memories (XP) with its heir — the donor is gone |
| Loss matters | Permadeath, graveyard, grief that lowers belonging |
| Growth through community | Evolution stages require both levels and Essence gathered by the colony |

## Cast (starter slimes)

- **Bonk** the Barbarian — loud, always hungry, secretly afraid of the dark.
- **Mizzle** the Mage — hoards shiny things; thinks Bonk is "unrefined".
- **Dr. Splorp** the Medic — worries about everyone, sleeps least of all.
- **Slip** the Rogue — borrows things. Returns them. Usually.
- **Pip** the Ranger — quiet, loyal, best nose for food.
- **Morrow** the Necromancer — polite, tea-obsessed, unnerving.

## Chapters (campaign hooks for future content)

1. **Wake Up, Goo** – first pulls; teach feeding, sleep, and a first hunt.
2. **Squabble Season** – first inter-slime conflict; the colony learns mediation.
3. **The Crust Road** – hunt zones expand; first elite battle.
4. **Echoes of the Kings** – evolution quests, legendary slimes tied to Hollow Kings' lore.
5. **The Heart of the Hollow** – final battle against the Dry Tide's avatar, **the Parched King**.

## Tone

Cozy-silly on the surface, with real melancholy underneath. Slimes talk in short, bouncy lines
("blorp!"); the Keeper's narration is dry and warm.
